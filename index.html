THE PHILOSOPHY

We design backwards from the fabric, not forwards from the trend.

Every Novique style starts in a mill, not a mood board. If a fabric can't survive five years of wear and one honest repair, it doesn't make the cut.

WHERE IT COMES FROM

Mills we've visited. Twice.

We work with eleven family-run mills across Italy, Japan, and Portugal — most of them smaller than our own studio. We visit each one before the first order, and again before the second, because a fabric that looks right on a hanger doesn't always wear right on a body.

Every garment carries a repair card, not a care label. Send it back in year three and we'll mend the elbow, not sell you a replacement.
